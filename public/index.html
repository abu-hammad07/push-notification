🔒 HTTPS Required on iPhone / Mobile
Apple Safari blocks Web Push notifications over plain HTTP (http://...). To test on iPhone:
1. Run an HTTPS tunnel on PC (e.g. npx localtunnel --port 4000).
2. Open the https:// URL in Safari.
3. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
⚠️ Notifications are Blocked in Browser
Default popup cannot open because permissions are blocked.
To show popup again:
1. Click the tune/lock icon 🔒 in your browser address bar (top-left).
2. Change Notifications to "Ask" or click "Reset permission".
3. Refresh this page, and the popup will show up automatically!
